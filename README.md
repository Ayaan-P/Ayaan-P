# Ayaan Pupala

## Fullstack / ML Engineer 

I studied Computer Engineering at UIUC, worked as a federal contractor in DC, and later joined a biotech startup developing deep learning models for early cancer detection. I completed my Master's in AI at Northwestern, refining my expertise in machine learning, automation, and product design.

I'm fascinated by how AI can enhance everyday experiences. This led me to create Dytto — a personal context platform that makes any AI know you like your best friend does. It continuously learns who you are and serves that context via API. The platform includes a journaling app where your context surfaces as beautiful daily stories, but the context engine is the core — any AI can plug in.
